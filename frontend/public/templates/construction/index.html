Milestones

Our Operational Roadmap

We divide our construction workflow into simple transparent phases, ensuring schedule coordination and structural excellence.

01

Blueprint Conceptualization

We analyze coordinates and client objectives, translating sketches into solid 3D digital twins to analyze spacing and structural integrity.

02

Pre-Construction Estimating

We calculate pricing models against concrete, steel structure heights, and finishing qualities to align the project within budget coordinates.

03

Active Site Mobilization

We establish site perimeter safety, deliver heavy machinery and crane grids, cast foundations, and begin the assembly timeline.

04

Superstructure Assembly

Erection of steel frames, column pours, interior buildout configurations, facade glass glazing, and final certification inspections.
